FAQ

Do you supply oil, nicotine liquid or THC?

No. This is a hardware-only service. I never source, handle or advise on THC oil, cannabis extracts or any controlled substance.

Who can I work with on cannabis hardware?

Only state-licensed US operators and federally licensed Canadian businesses operating legally in their jurisdiction. Proof of license may be requested before any work starts.

Do you guarantee regulatory approval?

No. The compliance checklist helps you ask the right questions (e.g. materials, labeling, battery and shipping requirements), but it isn't legal advice. Please confirm with qualified counsel in your market.

Are you a factory or agent taking commission?

Neither. I charge a fixed consulting fee, and any commercial relationship will be disclosed upfront.

Are the prices final?

Prices are indicative starting points. You'll get a fixed quote after the intro call.

Compliance note: Hardware consulting only. No THC oil, nicotine liquids or controlled substances. Cannabis-related work is limited to licensed clients in legal markets (state-licensed US, Canada). Clients are responsible for complying with local laws, including age restrictions.
